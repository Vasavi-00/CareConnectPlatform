## 2. Services Overview & User Roles
CareConnect provides an integrated digital care management system:
* **Family Dashboard:** Enables authorized family members and primary caregivers to monitor medication schedules, schedule and track clinical appointments, configure in-app and emergency notifications, and view wellness summaries generated by the AI Companion.
* **Elder Dashboard:** Provides an accessible, high-contrast, voice-assisted interface (supporting English and Telugu) allowing seniors to view daily doses, mark medicines as taken, view doctor visits, converse with the AI Companion, and initiate emergency SOS alerts.

## 6. AI Voice Companion & Speech Data
* **Supportive AI Nature:** The CareConnect AI Companion utilizes conversational models to offer companionship, check in on daily routines, and reduce senior isolation. Outputs are generated computationally and are not medical advice.
* **Audio Transcription:** Spoken audio is recorded via client-side MediaRecorder and transcribed (via Web Speech API or transcription backends) exclusively to enable conversational turns. Audio files are not monetized or sold to third-party advertisers.
* **Caregiver Summaries:** To keep families informed, non-confidential sentiment and daily health mentions (e.g., reported discomfort or appetite) are summarized for family review.
